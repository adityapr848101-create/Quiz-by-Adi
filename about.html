About Quiz by Adi

Empowering NEET, JEE & UPSC aspirants with free, powerful tools.

Our Mission

Quiz by Adi was created with a simple goal: to give every competitive exam aspirant in India — whether preparing for NEET, JEE, or UPSC — access to free, powerful, and easy-to-use tools that make studying and practicing more effective.

What We Offer

  • PDF Vertical Cut — Split two-column PDF pages vertically in one click.
  • Manual Horizontal Cut — Slice PDF pages horizontally with precision.
  • PDF to Quiz — Automatically convert any PDF into a custom quiz.
  • Quiz Maker — Build fully custom mock tests for self-assessment.
100% Free Forever. No signup, no login, no hidden charges. All tools run directly in your browser.
