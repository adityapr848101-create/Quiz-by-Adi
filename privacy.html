Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Short version: Your PDFs and files never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Quiz by Adi is designed with privacy first. We do not collect any personal information from users. Specifically:

  • We do not require signup, login, or any account.
  • We do not collect names, emails, or phone numbers.
  • We do not upload your PDFs or files to any server.
  • All file processing happens locally in your browser.

2. Local Browser Storage

Some tools may use your browser's local storage to save preferences (like theme or last-used settings). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services for functionality:

  • Google Fonts — for typography.
  • Font Awesome CDN — for icons.
  • GitHub Pages — for hosting.

These services may collect basic technical data (like IP address) as per their own privacy policies. We have no control over their data practices.

4. Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking. Any cookies set are from third-party services (like Google Fonts) and are strictly for functionality.

5. Children's Privacy

Our tools are safe for users of all ages, including students under 13. Since we collect no personal data, there is no risk to minors.

6. Your Rights

Since we collect no data, there is nothing to delete or export. You have full control over your files at all times.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us on Telegram or visit our Contact page.
